# Media credits and publication status

## Models and textures

All three supplied GLBs and Blender source files are preserved byte-for-byte,
along with their original preview images and the speaker cabinet texture.

The original vinyl material referenced Unsplash filenames by Donald Giannatti
(`donald-giannatti-7lbzwVoEh9c-unsplash.jpg`) and an unused image by Gracious Adebayo
(`gracious-adebayo-zAPbXrhIfTU-unsplash.jpg`). Those original image dependencies
were missing from the Blender archive; artwork was embedded in the vinyl GLB.
The exact photo pages could not be verified during cleanup.

The earlier cleanup's replacement label has been fully reverted at the author's
request. The original embedded artwork, thumbnail and unmodified Blender sources
are restored. No generated replacement label remains. Original modelling
screenshots are retained; test screenshots containing academic identifiers have
been refreshed from the restored app. See CHANGES.md for their exact scope.

Unsplash imagery is licensed rather than automatically public domain; its
[licence](https://unsplash.com/license) and [terms](https://unsplash.com/terms)
also distinguish rights in photographs from rights in depicted people/brands/art.
Restoring the artwork is not a verification of its redistribution permissions.
